# Why should you teach others

*Section: Mockito Basics*

Instructor: Congratulations on achieving another wonderful milestone. You're making a lot of progress. One of my recommendations for every learner out there is that you should start teaching others. Why should you start teaching others? Because it enhances understanding. Teaching a concept can deepen your understanding of a subject. It improves your communication skills as well. Understanding technology is important, but how do you convey your ideas?

That's also very, very important. When you teach others, you are trying to simplify complex ideas, and this would improve your communication skills. Next is that it builds confidence. By sharing knowledge, you're boosting yourself esteem, and you're boosting your authority on a subject. It also encourages learning. I see that I learn a lot about a topic when I'm trying to create a course on it. A lot of times I think I'm an expert on a topic, and when I'm creating a course, I learn that I missed a lot of important things.

So preparing to teach also helps you to learn a lot. Preparing to teach requires staying updated, and it also expands your knowledge base, and most importantly, it fosters empathy. Whenever I'm teaching, I need to think from the perspective of the learner. What is the learner thinking, what is his perspective? So when you are teaching, you would try and understand others' perspectives, and you would adapt your teaching methods to that, and this would help you to build empathy.

So always remember, when you teach, you learn twice. Let me congratulate you again on reaching another milestone, and I'll see you again very, very soon.
