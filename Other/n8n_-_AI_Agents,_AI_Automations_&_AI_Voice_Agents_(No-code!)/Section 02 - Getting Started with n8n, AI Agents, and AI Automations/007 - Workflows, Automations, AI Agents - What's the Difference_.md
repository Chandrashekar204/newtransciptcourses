# Workflows, Automations, AI Agents - What's the Difference?

*Section: Getting Started with n8n, AI Agents, and AI Automations*

In the last video, we talk about what is and why it's so powerful. Now let's dive into some key concepts you need to understand automations, workflows, and AI agents. Let's start with automation. So what is automation? This is simply any process where tasks happen on their own without you having to do them manually. Think of setting an email rule that moves all invoices to a specific folder. The key benefits are that automations save time, avoid mistakes, and handle routine optimization while you focus on more important things.

They follow specifically planned actions in a set sequence. Now, a workflow is how you actually build that automation. It's your visual recipe, the blueprint that maps out your process step by step. So how it works. You start with a trigger. Like for example, when I receive an email, then connect different actions like saving to Google Sheets, sending a slack notification, and so on. Whatever you need in Naa10, you build these workflows visually by dragging and connecting different nodes.

Basically, think of a workflow as the blueprint that maps out your automation process step by step. Agents are completely different. They are intelligent tools that use artificial intelligence to make decisions. Understand language and conduct conversations autonomously. The key difference is that automation equals specifically planned actions that follow rules, while agents equal thinking plus decisions plus autonomy.

So what can agents do? For example, they can analyze messages and understand context. They can understand user intentions behind requests. Answer complex questions intelligently and adapt to new situations and changing information. Here is a perfect example. An agent can read customer emails, understands what they are really asking, then responds with the right answer or redirects to the appropriate team member.

Therefore, unlike standard automations that follow simple if this, then that rules agents can think, adapt, and make intelligent decisions on their own. In short, automations follow the plan. Workflows are the blueprints, and agents can think for themselves. In the next lesson, you will create your own account and explore the interface. So let's dive in.
