# What should I do when I face a challenge

I'm frequently asked this question, what should I do when I face a challenge? What should I do when I am stuck? Number one, try harder. I would recommend you to try and troubleshoot the problem on your own. I would recommend you to resist the urge to immediately reach out for help.

Solving problems on your own builds resilience and deepens your understanding. Number two is ask Google so you can dive into a research mode and search on Google. If you got an error message, type it out on Google. There's high chance that somebody else has encountered this error and you might be able to find a solution as well. Number three is ask ChatGPT. Generative AI is making rapid strides and generative AI tools are really, really good.

Just remember that ChatGPT is not always accurate, but most of the times it gives you a good answer, so make sure that you type in your question clearly and concisely. And ChatGPT can provide you with explanations, quote snippets, and it can even provide you with debugging tips. However, always remember that ChatGPT is not accurate, and also be really, really cautious with your data. Don't share any sensitive or personal information when seeking help from ChatGPT.

If these three tips did not help you, it's time to ask for help in Q and A. You can go ahead and post your question in the course discussion board. Our entire team, along with your fellow students are ready to help you. However, it's very, very important to remember that the more details you provide, the easier it is for someone to help you. So post as many details as possible. Number five, do not forget to give back.

If you see a question and you know the answer, go ahead and share your knowledge. Teaching others is a powerful way to enhance your own learning. Last but not the least, when everything does not work, it's time to take a break, take a walk, or take a nap. Countless times I woke up the next morning with a solution to a problem that was bugging me the previous evening.
